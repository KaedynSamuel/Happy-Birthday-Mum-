Dear Mum,

Happy Birthday, Mum There aren’t enough words in this world to truly express how grateful I am for you, but today I want to try. I may not have given you a big gift, but please know that my love and appreciation for you go far beyond anything material. You are the greatest blessing in my life, and I carry that truth with me every single day. You are more than just my mother you are my guide, my strength, my protector, and my safe place. You have been the constant light in my life, even in moments when things felt dark. I wish you could see yourself the way I see you the strength you carry, the love you give so freely, and the quiet sacrifices you make that often go unnoticed. Some of my favourite memories aren’t the big moments, but the small ones the times we dance together without a care in the world, laughing like nothing else matters. The countless times you’ve sat there and listened to me talk and talk, even when I know I was probably yapping non stop… and you still gave me your full attention, your patience, and your love. And of course… our flapjacks. The simple moments where I make them for you and seeing how much you love them it means more to me than you probably realise. Those little things, those everyday moments, are what I hold closest to my heart. When I see you happy, it fills my heart in a way I can’t even describe. And when you’re hurting, I feel it deeply, because you mean that much to me. I want you to always remember that you are never alone I will always have your back, just as you have always had mine. I truly could not have asked for a better mother. You are extraordniary. The way you hold this family together, the way you love us unconditionally, and the way you continue to give even when you’re tired it inspires me every single day. You are the reason I wake up and push myself to be better. Everything I strive to become is rooted in the example you’ve set. Thank you for your endless compassion. Thank you for your strength when things are difficult. Thank you for your patience, your wisdom, and your unwavering love. You are the heart of this family the glue that keeps us together. You bring peace into our lives, joy into our days, and laughter into our home. Your presence alone makes everything feel okay. I need you to know this clearly you are appreciated more than words can say. You are loved more than you may ever fully realise. And you are admired not just as a mother, but as an incredible human being. No matter what happens in this world, I will always stand by you. I would do anything just to see you happy, because your happiness means everything to me. Mum, you are my greatest blessing, my biggest inspiration, and my forever home. Happy Birthday. I love you more than words will ever be able to express. ❤️
